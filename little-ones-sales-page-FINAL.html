How quickly will I receive my book? +

Immediately. Once you pay and enter your child's details, the book is generated and your PDF is ready to download in under two minutes. There is no waiting.

Can I print it at home? +

Yes — the PDF is A5 format, designed to print beautifully on home or professional printers. You can also read it on any phone, tablet, or computer screen.

Is the story really unique to my child? +

Yes. Every book is freshly written using your child's specific details — their name, age, gender, and what makes them special. No two books are identical.

What ages is this suitable for? +

The system calibrates to the age you enter — from toddlers (age 2) all the way to age 12. The language, story complexity, and prayer tone all adapt accordingly.

Can I order as a gift for someone else's child? +

Absolutely. Simply enter the recipient child's details. You'll receive the PDF and can forward or print it as a gift. It makes an extraordinary personalised present.

What payment methods are accepted? +

We accept all major Nigerian bank cards, bank transfers, and international cards via Selar's secure payment platform.
